Set up in three steps.

  1. On your Mac, install Bridge and open Codex. Allow Bluetooth and grant Bridge Accessibility access.
  2. Open Trackypady on your iPhone and scan the Bridge QR, or use iPhone Camera and tap the banner.
  3. Double tap with three fingers to bring Codex forward. Hold three fingers to dictate; release to finish.

Trackypady for iPhone is being prepared for App Store release. Its on-phone gesture demo can be tried without a Mac. Live controls require a working Codex desktop installation/account, provided separately.
